Support

A question, a problem or a suggestion about OPTIMAC?

Contact us

Open a support request. For a technical issue, attach the redacted diagnostic: Settings > Privacy > Redacted diagnostic > Export… It contains no file name or personal path.

Why does OPTIMAC ask for my home folder?

Mac App Store apps can only read the folders you choose. You select it once; access is then remembered. Nothing is changed without your confirmation.

Space isn’t freed after a cleanup

OPTIMAC moves items to the Trash so they stay recoverable. The space is returned to the disk once the Trash is emptied, or through “Delete permanently” in OPTIMAC for only the items it placed there.

Restore an item

Open the Trash in the Finder, select the item, then choose “Put Back”.

Restore my purchases

Settings > Subscription > Restore purchases, with the same Apple Account used for the purchase.

Manage or cancel my subscription

In the App Store: your name at the bottom left > Account Settings > Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your data stays on your Mac.

Uninstall OPTIMAC

Move OPTIMAC to the Trash from the Applications folder, then delete the folder ~/Library/Containers/fr.kmcapitalgroup.optimac to erase its data.

FAQ

Good questions.

Can Optiwise erase an important file?

No. Optiwise never deletes permanently: it moves the items you chose to the Trash, after a preview. Documents, photos, secrets, databases, backups, synced data and Git-tracked files are protected by default and never suggested.

Does my data leave my Mac?

No. The whole scan runs on your Mac. No file name, path or content is sent, and the app contains no telemetry. Details are in our privacy policy.

Which version of macOS do I need?

macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on Apple silicon or Intel Macs. Local AI explanations need macOS 26 and an Apple Intelligence-compatible Mac; without them, Optiwise uses pre-written explanations.

What’s left if I stop Pro?

Everything free keeps working, and your profiles, rules and history stay on your Mac. Only new advanced scans go back to being Pro-only.

How do I cancel my subscription?

From the App Store: click your name, then “Account Settings” and “Subscriptions”. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period.

Why does Optiwise ask for access to some folders?

macOS asks for your permission before Optiwise can measure your Documents, Desktop and Downloads. If you decline, the scan carries on and the category is simply marked “Access denied”. Full Disk Access is optional.

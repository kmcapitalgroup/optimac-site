What Optiwise never touches

  • Your documents, photos, videos, music and projects
  • Mail, Messages, Safari and iCloud Drive
  • Keychains, SSH keys, certificates and .env files
  • App preferences and containers protected by macOS
  • Databases, backups and libraries (Photos, Lightroom, Final Cut, Logic…)
  • Git-tracked files and project folders you didn’t choose

Privacy Policy

Optiwise scans your Mac locally. Here is exactly what is processed, and what never is. Last updated: October 28, 2026.

No data collected

Optiwise collects no personal data and sends nothing to KM Capital Group or any third party. The app has no network access at all: it cannot send data, even by mistake.

Local processing

Storage, cache, project, application and startup scans run on your Mac. No file content, file name or path is sent to a server.

Data kept on your Mac

Settings, profiles, rules, the last scan report and history are saved in Optiwise’s container on your Mac. History contains only masked paths and is kept for the duration you choose (30 days to 1 year).

Folder access

Optiwise only reads the folders you allow in a macOS panel. No file is moved without a preview and your confirmation, and only to the Trash.

Local AI

When Apple’s on-device models are available, only categories and sizes are passed to them, locally. The AI can neither delete nor modify files.

Subscriptions

Purchases are handled by Apple’s App Store. Optiwise neither receives nor stores payment data.

Statistics

No telemetry or usage statistics are built in. Any change would require explicit consent and would be off by default.

Your rights

All data stays on your Mac. You can clear the history from the app at any time, and delete everything by deleting the folder ~/Library/Containers/fr.kmcapitalgroup.optimac.

Controller

KM Capital Group, publisher of Optiwise. For any question: support page.
